What data we collect

Information you give us

  • Discovery call bookings on our contact page: your name, email address, the service you are interested in, any message you add, the time slot you choose and your time zone.
  • Emails, calls and messages you send us, including anything you share about your project.
  • Client project information when we work together, such as business contact details, requirements and files you provide. This is also covered by our contract with you.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: when you visit, our hosting provider records technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Font requests: our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it downloads them. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • Your cookie choice, stored in your own browser. See our Cookie Policy.
  • Google Maps on our contact page loads only if you allow embedded content. Google may then set cookies and receive your IP address.

We do not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking tools, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

Who we share data with

We never sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as far as they need it:

  • Netlify, Inc. (USA): hosts this website, receives booking form submissions and keeps server logs.
  • Google LLC (USA): Google Fonts, Google Maps (only with your consent), and Google Workspace tools such as Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Meet that we use to email you and hold calls.
  • Freelance marketplaces such as Fiverr and Upwork, only if you choose to hire us there. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Professional advisers and authorities (for example accountants or a court) when the law requires it.

International transfers

We are based in Bangladesh and our providers operate in the USA and other countries, so your data may be processed outside your own country, including outside the EU, UK or USA. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our providers' Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified, or other lawful safeguards.

How long we keep data

  • Booking requests and enquiries that don't become a project: up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client and project records: for the length of our work together, then as long as tax and accounting law requires (usually up to 6 years).
  • Server logs: for the limited period set by our hosting provider.
  • Your cookie choice: 12 months in your browser, then we ask again.

  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including for direct marketing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example in , without affecting earlier processing;

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't use sensitive personal information. You can ask to know, correct or delete your information, and we won't treat you differently for doing so.

To use any of these rights, email info@techfiyr.com. We will reply within one month (or sooner where the law requires) and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

How we protect your data

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS. We limit access to personal data to the team members who need it, use reputable providers with strong security, and protect our accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites
